# Local SEO Evidence, Policy, and Operating Model

## Source Key & Evidence Standard

- **Confirmed**: Platform documentation (Google, Apple, Microsoft) or regulatory rules (FTC)
- **Study**: Empirical research from recognized industry analysts (Whitespark, Sterling Sky, BrightLocal)
- **Consensus**: Common practitioner working agreement without controlled platform confirmation
- **Caution**: Single-source or unverified industry claims

Every recommendation should carry an evidence label:
- `[Confirmed Platform Guidance]`: Documented platform rule or official policy
- `[Practitioner Interpretation]`: Empirical study heuristic (not a causal platform formula)
- `[Unverified Folklore]`: Debunked tactic or unproven speculation

## Google Local Ranking Concepts & Controllability Matrix

Google confirms local results depend primarily on **Relevance**, **Distance**, and **Prominence** (Confirmed). Google publishes no numerical weights.

| Factor / Input | Controllability Level | Operating Response |
|---|---|---|
| Business model & staffed location | Constrained | Represent reality accurately. Never manufacture fake locations. |
| Searcher distance | Mostly not controlled | Measure geographic variation. Focus on realistic service radius. |
| Profile completeness & categories | Controlled | Keep primary and secondary categories, hours, and attributes exact. |
| Website usefulness & conversion path | Controlled | Build helpful, distinct local service pages with clear contact paths. |
| Customer experience | Influenced | Improve operations and close feedback loops. |
| Review volume & positive ratings | Influenced | Ask all customers neutrally. Never incentivize or gate reviews. |
| Relevant local references | Influenced | Earn references from chambers, suppliers, partners, and community. |
| Algorithm changes & SERP layouts | Not controlled | Diversify organic and listing discovery. Avoid single-tactic reliance. |

## Internal Business-Facts Record (11 Fields)

To prevent operational drift across platforms, maintain one internal source of truth:

1. **Real-World Business Name**: Exact customer-facing legal/signage name (no SEO stuffing)
2. **Business Model**: Storefront, Service Area Business (SAB), Hybrid, or Multi-location
3. **Address Visibility**: Public street address vs hidden address per platform rules
4. **Primary Phone**: Location-controlled line with call-tracking configuration documented
5. **Website URL**: Clean location destination URL with documented UTM tracking convention
6. **Regular & Special Hours**: Standard hours plus documented holiday review owner and dates
7. **Categories**: Primary category ("this business is a") plus 3-5 specific secondary categories
8. **Services & Products**: Accurate offer catalog matching visible website landing pages
9. **Service Areas**: Realistic operating boundaries (cities/zips where staff actually travel)
10. **Profile Ownership**: Primary owner account, active managers, recovery contacts documented
11. **Change Log**: Date, editor, before value, after value, rationale, and verification outcome

## Review Compliance: Google Policy & FTC Enforcement

- **Zero-Incentive Rule (Google Maps Policy)**: Google strictly prohibits offering payment, discounts, gift cards, contest entries, free services, or refunds in exchange for reviews (Confirmed). Google's policy is stricter than general commercial law.
- **Review Gating Prohibited**: Pre-screening customers (e.g. asking "Were you happy?" and sending only satisfied customers to Google) violates Google policy and FTC regulations (Confirmed).
- **FTC Consumer Reviews Rule (Effective Oct 21, 2024)**: Prohibits fake reviews, sentiment-conditioned incentives, and review suppression. Penalties up to $53,088 per violation (Confirmed).
- **Owner Review Replies**: Respond for customer service and dispute resolution, not keyword stuffing. Google does not publish keyword replies as a ranking factor (Confirmed). Respect HIPAA in healthcare (never confirm/deny patient relationship) and attorney-client privilege in legal.

## Location Pages: The City-Name Swap Test

Google spam policies classify regional pages that funnel users to one destination without distinct value as doorway abuse (Confirmed).

| Criteria | Useful Location Page | Thin City-Swap Page (Doorway Risk) |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Serves a real location or distinct service area | Exists solely to capture a city keyword |
| Content | Operationally unique facts (staff, fleet, hours) | Generic boilerplate with city tokens swapped |
| Usability | Helps customer visit, book, or verify coverage | Funnels every visitor to identical generic form |
| Structure | Integrated into browseable locator hierarchy | Orphaned landing pages linked only from footer |
| Maintenance | Updated when local staff, hours, or services change | Auto-generated at scale without local ownership |

**The Swap Test**: If swapping the city name with an adjacent city leaves the page content substantially true, the page lacks local value and carries doorway deindexing risk.

## Local SEO Myths vs Evidence

| Claim / Tactic | Evidence Verdict | Better Action |
|---|---|---|
| Add city/service keywords to GBP name | Conflicts with Google real-world name policy | Use real name; put services in dedicated fields |
| Post on GBP daily to rank higher | No confirmed ranking cadence | Post when genuine customer updates exist |
| Geotag photos for a Maps boost | Unverified folklore (no ranking effect) | Publish clear, representative, rights-cleared photos |
| Put target keywords in review replies | Not documented as a ranking factor | Reply helpfully, calmly, and protect privacy |
| Embed a Google Map to rank in pack | No direct ranking effect (Confirmed) | Embed map if it genuinely assists customer navigation |
| Match NAP punctuation identically everywhere | Overstated as direct factor | Fix material errors (wrong phone, wrong address, closed site) |
| Build a fixed 100 citations target | No universal count published | Prioritize Tier 1 platforms, data aggregators, industry hubs |
| LocalBusiness schema guarantees pack rank | Structured data is not a ranking guarantee | Mark up visible facts accurately; validate syntax |
| Publish a page for every nearby town | High doorway abuse and spam risk | Build pages only where distinct operations or staff exist |
| Incentivize reviews with discounts | Prohibited by Google; FTC violation risk | Ask all eligible customers neutrally with official link |
| Rank grid score equals revenue impact | Diagnostic snapshot only | Measure calls, direction requests, bookings, and revenue |
